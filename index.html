dotclipforge

A personal desktop tool that edits authorized stream highlights into vertical clips and sends them to TikTok as drafts.

How it works

  1. The owner adds a stream recording they are authorized to use, with the permission of the streamer or under the rules of a creator campaign.
  2. The tool finds the highlights, edits them into 9:16 clips with subtitles and a credit to the original creator.
  3. The owner reviews every clip and approves the ones to keep.
  4. Approved clips are uploaded to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts, through TikTok's official Content Posting API.
  5. The owner opens each draft in the TikTok app, checks the caption and publishes it manually.

dotclipforge never publishes on its own and has no users other than its owner.
