1. What dotclipforge is

dotclipforge is a personal desktop tool operated by Tristan M. It edits stream recordings into short vertical clips and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered as a service to the public, and it has no other users.

2. Content

The tool only processes recordings the owner is authorized to use, for example with the written permission of the streamer or under the rules of a creator campaign. Each clip credits the original creator. The owner is responsible for the content they choose to publish and for complying with the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Publishing

dotclipforge never publishes on its own. Clips are uploaded as drafts to the TikTok inbox of the connected account. Publishing is a manual action by the owner in the TikTok app.

4. TikTok account access

Access to TikTok goes through TikTok's official authorization (Login Kit) and Content Posting API, with the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload only. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app. Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy.

The tool is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
